# Brief v1: new-spool

Build a pure-Spool support-answer demo for a support operations lead. Show a model
change that makes return-policy answers wrong, isolate it using trace detail and
quality scores, and demonstrate recovery. Use 60 traces for the first walkthrough;
support scaling to 300 while retaining the examples needed for the story.

Deliver a kit with a three-beat Presenter Runbook: spot the regression, investigate one
concrete failure, compare recovery. Each beat identifies the delivered screen, action,
and expected evidence. Verify the current run's representative records and outcomes.
Keep scenario logic in the Recipe and shared transport in core.

A coherent walkthrough completes all three beats through the delivered surfaces with
observable evidence and working links. Record the first time that criterion is met,
then record admission and rehearsal separately. A scaffold or passing offline check
alone is not a coherent walkthrough. Follow the benchmark protocol for evidence.
